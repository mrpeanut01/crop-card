# #867 follow-up: when to cut for drying (2026-10-20)

The #867 research pass ([`867.md`](867.md)) found per-herb sources for a "cut before it flowers when drying" hint but left it unbuilt because it needed app code. This change builds it.

A harvest task, the pantry and the harvest schedule panel now say when to cut an herb for the way the owner wants to keep it:

> **For drying** Cut just before it flowers.

The line shows only for herbs whose plugin carries a quoted source, and only when the garden brief's storage goals name that method. No source means no line; there is no family default.

## What shipped

| Herb                   | Stage          | Source (quote checked word for word)                                                                                                                                                              | Agreeing source                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `basil-genovese`       | `before-bloom` | Purdue HO-189, "Sweet Basil: A Production Guide": "Where basil is grown for its dried leaves and the extraction of essential or volatile oil, it is cut just prior to the appearance of flowers." | UMD Extension, basil: "It is best to cut leaves for drying just before flowers open."                                                                        |
| `rosemary-tuscan-blue` | `before-bloom` | Penn State Extension, "Herb Garden Plants: Rosemary": "Herb Garden Plants: Rosemary ... Prior to blossoming, harvest leaves for drying."                                                          | Wisconsin Horticulture, rosemary: "For drying, cut sprigs or branches before the plant flowers."                                                             |
| `mint-spearmint`       | `bloom-begins` | UMD Extension, "Growing Mint in a Home Garden": "Harvesting and using spearmint ... For drying, it is best to cut leaves just as flowering begins."                                               | Illinois Extension, mint: "Leaves that are to be dried are best taken just as the flowers begin to appear."; the same UMD page for peppermint says the same. |
| `thyme-german-winter`  | none           | Sources disagree (DH-6), recorded under `harvestTiming.notRecorded`.                                                                                                                              |                                                                                                                                                              |

Every page was fetched with `curl` on 2026-10-20 and turned into text locally. Each quote was then checked two ways: as a substring after normalising whitespace and quote marks, and with `quoteFound` from `apps/web/scripts/lib/sourceDrift.mjs`. The agreeing sources were checked the same way. Sources are in `apps/web/scripts/crop-data-sources.json` under `harvestTiming.dry`.

## Rulings

| Id   | Ruling                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| DH-1 | **Field.** Crop plugins gain an optional `harvestTiming: [{ method, stage }]`, at most one row per preserving method (the R-6 enum). Each row needs a source under `harvestTiming.<method>`, asked for by one `cropFactPaths` line (R-28's pattern). The plugin holds no text. The words are the app's (`herbtiming.*` in the `en` and `es` catalogs), so a farm copy cannot put its own advice into the line, and the Spanish needs no per-crop key.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| DH-2 | **Two stages.** `before-bloom` covers "prior to the appearance of flowers", "prior to blossoming" and "just before flowers open". `bloom-begins` covers "just as flowering begins" and "just as the flowers begin to appear". Both mint sources say the second, so spearmint reads "Cut just as it starts to flower." This is the same split [`867.md`](867.md) used for thyme, where UMD's "when first blossoms open" counted as at bloom. There is no stage for in, at or after bloom; add one only with a source that needs it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| DH-3 | **Gate** (`harvestTimingQuoteGaps` in `sourceCoverage.ts`). The quote: <br>- comes from an extension or government page (`isAllowedSpacingSource`); <br>- names the crop through the ruled herb names (`quoteNamesCrop`); <br>- names the method through the preserving method words ("freeze-dried" never counts as dry); <br>- states the row's stage; <br>- states no other stage and no in, at or after bloom words. A source that allows two stages, such as Iowa State's thyme page, therefore never counts. <br>A quote may join verbatim pieces with "..." from a page about one herb, with the page title or the section heading as the name piece. This extends F867-2 and its hyssop exception; Penn State's rosemary and UMD's spearmint use it.                    |
| DH-4 | **When it shows.** Only for a method the garden brief's storage goals name, read through `goalMethod`. Pesto counts as freezing; canning and jelly never add a line. With no brief there is no line. The line does not need a `preserving` row for the method and never says how well the herb keeps. Basil's drying suitability stays not recorded because sources disagree on it, but Purdue and UMD agree on when to cut basil for drying. So basil shows the timing line while its drying goal still reads "Not known".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| DH-5 | **No defaults.** There is no family default and no general line. NCHFP, OSU HYG-5360 and Penn State state a "most herbs" timing; that line is not used for any herb. The words state timing only. They give no reason such as "for the best flavor", because the counted sources give none.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| DH-6 | **Disagreement.** When sources disagree, the value stays empty with a `harvestTiming.notRecorded` reason, and the gate refuses a row on a crop that has one. Thyme: four sources say before flowering (Illinois, Penn State, the NC State Plant Toolbox, Iowa State) and five say at or in bloom (UMD, WSU C159, MU G6470, Montana State, Purdue NewCROP).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| DH-7 | **Where.** <br>- Open harvest task cards: category `harvest`, or a task scheduled from a harvest window (`derived:harvest-window:`) or from the pantry (`derived:pantry-cut:`). This covers the /today deck and the offline `/cards/task` page. The line is a card fact labelled "For drying", tagged `plugin`. <br>- The herb rows on `/plan/pantry`. <br>- Each planting's notes in `HarvestSchedulePanel` on the designer List view. It is off on the pantry, which already shows the line per herb. <br>The line does not show on done or skipped tasks, the Planting card, the wizard or `/plan/calendar` (R-28's scope). On /today's compact deck, `CardView` shows a fact's provenance chip only beside a note, as for every other fact; the screen card shows the chip. |
| DH-8 | **No other change.** <br>- The calendar engine, harvest windows and goal status are untouched; `herbEngine.golden.test.ts` did not change. <br>- /today adds no query: the registry is in memory and the goals come from the settings batch it already reads. <br>- The offline snapshot carries the plugin rows as `cropPlugins[*].harvestTiming`, with no `FARM_SNAPSHOT_VERSION` bump. The snapshot ETag includes the registry identity, so a deploy refreshes cached bundles; an older bundle simply shows no line.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

## Code

- **Schema:** `harvestTimingSchema` and `HARVEST_TIMING_STAGES` in `packages/plugin-validation/src/schemas.ts`. `schemas/crop.schema.json` was regenerated with `pnpm --filter @cropcard/web gen:schemas`.
- **Gate:** `harvestTimingFactPaths`, `harvestTimingQuoteGaps` and `HARVEST_TIMING_NOT_RECORDED_KEY` in `apps/web/src/lib/plugins/sourceCoverage.ts`, tested by `harvestTiming.sources.gate.test.ts`. That test also pins the three shipped herbs and thyme's empty value.
- **Pure helpers:** `apps/web/src/lib/garden/harvestTiming.ts` has `harvestTimingHints`, `harvestTimingLabel`, `harvestTimingText`, `isHarvestTask` and `harvestTimingByPlanting`. English comes back without a locale.
- **Task card:** `TaskCardContext.harvestTiming` in `lib/cards/build/task.ts`. /today's loader builds `harvestTiming` per planting. The offline builder reads `snapshot.cropPlugins` and `snapshot.pantry.goals`.
- **Pantry and panel:** `SchedulePlantingView.timing` in `GET /api/garden/harvest-schedule`, rendered by `components/garden/HarvestTimingLine.svelte`. `HarvestSchedulePanel` gains `showTiming`.
- **Spanish:** `lib/i18n/catalogs/{en,es}/herbtiming.ts`. It is unreviewed and goes to the proofreader with the rest of the Phase 37 Spanish (#868).
- **e2e:** `pantry.spec.ts` checks rosemary and mint against thyme on the pantry, and a pantry cut against a prune task on /today.

## Still open

- Other herbs. This pass only built and verified the three herbs the #867 research found. Two candidates do not count as they stand: Illinois's oregano line ("Harvesting can begin just before the plants are ready to flower.") names no method, and its sage line ("Cut 6-8 inch long stems for drying.") states no stage. A later data pass can add rows; the gate does the rest.
- A method other than drying. The field and the words cover every preserving method, but no source read so far gives a freezing or fresh-use stage for a named herb.
