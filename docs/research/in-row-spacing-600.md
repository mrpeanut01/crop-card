# Crop in-row spacing sources (#600)

Every crop plugin's `plantingGuide.inRowSpacingIn` now has a verbatim quote from an allowed .edu or .gov source in `apps/web/scripts/crop-data-sources.json`, or was removed. `cropFactPaths` asks for the source on every crop, and `rowSpacingGaps` (through `inRowSpacingQuoteGaps` in `apps/web/src/lib/plugins/sourceCoverage.ts`) checks three things: the host is .edu, .gov or UF/IFAS EDIS (`journals.flvc.org`), so seed-company pages fail (#591 ruling Q3); the quote states both ends of the stored range (inches, feet, fractions such as 3/4 or 3½, or a number word); and, off tree crops, the quote names the in-row axis ("between plants", "in the row", "apart", "thin", "within a row", "in all directions", a "Plants" column).

Result: 234 crops sourced (190 of them with a changed value) and 111 removed. The 15 tree crops keep the #591 sources. Every quoted fragment was checked against the fetched source text.

A crop with no in-row value falls to the 12 in `FALLBACK_SPACING_IN` placeholder, tagged `fallback` (plant counts read `fallback` too). The layout engine now carries the tag as well: `footprintOf` in `lib/layout/sufficiency.ts` reports `fallback` when a footprint comes from an unsourced spacing, `withSpacingProvenance` marks those engine and AI assignments `spacingProvenance: 'fallback'`, and the wizard Review step shows the Provenance tag next to the plant count (Invariant 7).

## Panel rulings (2026-10-06)

Panel: farmer/agronomist, data integrity, precedent/engineering.

| #   | Question                                                           | Ruling                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Vote                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Q1  | Sources give a range; the field holds a range                      | Store the source's printed range in inches. A single figure or an "at least X" is min = max = X. For the overlap test an "at least X" or "no closer than X" figure is the range X and up                   | 3-0 (overlap reading 2-0, one silent) |
| Q2  | "Sow 1-2 in, thin to 6 in"                                         | Store the final spacing after thinning. A sowing figure with no thinning step is the final spacing                                                                                                         | 3-0                                   |
| Q3  | Axis wording                                                       | "Plant spacing", "Distance Between Plants", "plants N in apart" count as in-row; a bare "Spacing" column or an unlabelled "A by B" (VCE 426-403/408 crop pubs) does not (#587 R2)                          | 3-0                                   |
| Q4  | Non-overlapping sources                                            | #551 applied strictly: any counted allowed source that does not overlap leaves the value empty                                                                                                             | 3-0                                   |
| Q4a | Commercial figures                                                 | Count when they are for the same crop and system; drilled, population, planter-table and "highest yields" study figures do not                                                                             | 3-0                                   |
| Q4b | Trellised, high-tunnel, seed-grown, day-neutral, baby-leaf figures | Do not count against a crop not grown that way; do count when the plugin is that system (Albion is day-neutral)                                                                                            | 3-0                                   |
| Q4c | A figure for a different type of the crop                          | Does not count (spring radish vs daikon, crown vs seed-sown rhubarb, cut-flower vs giant sunflower)                                                                                                        | 3-0                                   |
| Q4d | County pages, newsletters, blogs on allowed hosts                  | Count, both as sources and as disagreements                                                                                                                                                                | 3-0                                   |
| Q5  | VCE 426-840 contradicting itself                                   | Empty when the table and the text do not overlap (blueberry). Strawberry June-bearing takes the text's matted-row 18-24 in, which sits inside the table minimum                                            | 3-0 (strawberry 2-1)                  |
| Q6  | Hosts                                                              | UC ANR Master Gardener pages, UF/IFAS county blogs, EDIS and Purdue NewCROP count; hortscans.ces.ncsu.edu scans of other institutions' newsletters do not                                                  | 3-0 (hortscans 2-1)                   |
| Q7  | Crop-type matches                                                  | Komatsuna, mizuna and tatsoi do not take VCE "Mustard" (3-0); bok choy and napa take "Cabbage, Chinese" (3-0); dry beans take "Beans, bush" (3-0); Triple Crown takes the "erect and semierect" text (2-1) | as shown                              |
| Q8  | Microgreens and drilled or broadcast crops                         | No in-row value: removed (area mode where a sourced seedingRate exists, else the tagged placeholder)                                                                                                       | 3-0                                   |

Per-crop calls on disputed crops went by majority. Ship 2-1: rhubarb (NDSU 3-4 ft for crowns), ground cherry, luffa, ProCut sunflower. Empty 2-1: celosia (the UMD figure is for cut-flower plumes), Concord, Niagara and Marquette grapes (Cornell 8 ft), Pink Lemonade blueberry, ginger, turmeric, scallions. The data-integrity panelist also cross-checked the VCE Table 5 crops against two allowed tables already fetched for #591 (Cornell "Recommended Spacing ... in New York" and the NC State planting calendar); under Q4 the coordinator applied the non-overlaps found there (kale, watermelon, mustard greens, spinach, garlic, bush beans, snow pea, peanut, sunchoke). Sea buckthorn's only figure is metric ("1 m"), so the stored inch value could not be checked against the quote; it is empty.
