# Spray Cards on small paper (#581)

The #572 print sweep found that on 4x6 and 3x5 index stock, and on Letter 4-up, a Spray Card whose sprayer needs decon first printed the decon SOP first, and then the EPA number, rate, per-tank amount, REI, PHI and mix order faded out under the card's overflow mask. A "RATE" label could print without its value. Deciding what a Spray Card may drop is a safety call, so a panel ruled on it on 2026-10-05.

## Panel

Three panelists, each reading the code and primary sources: a small-plot farmer and applicator (Loudoun County VA), a pesticide safety and regulatory reviewer (FIFRA §12(a)(2)(G), 40 CFR 170.503 and 170.507 Worker Protection Standard, WCAG 2.2), and a print and front-end engineer (CSS paged media, MDN `appearance`, Playwright). Rulings are by majority; every ruling below was 3-0.

## As built

- `lib/cards/build/spray.ts` marks the core facts (`CardFact.core`), orders them first (Rate, Per N-gal tank, REI, PHI, EPA reg. no., then Sprayer, Tank covers, Target), marks a required decon section `ownCard`, and sets `printWhole`, `printRepeatNotices` (the recheck notice) and `printRef` (the EPA reg. no.). Calibrate-first cards are `printWhole` too.
- `lib/cards/printPack.ts` `wholePrintParts(card, layout, ctx)` turns a whole-print card into numbered cards (`CardModel.printPart`) on 4x6, 3x5 and Letter 4-up. Heights come from `wrapLines` (greedy word wrap at a padded average glyph width) against the sizes in `WHOLE_PRINT`, which match CardView's `.whole` print styles; the QR and short URL sit in a 0.7 in rail at the right. Letter full pages and every other card kind are unchanged.
- `CardPrintSheet` expands whole-print cards before paginating; `CardView` renders a part with "Card N of M" in the kicker row, no safety hoisting, no fade and no "Cut short?" line, and the continued line. The card page says "Prints as N numbered cards on this paper" when a Spray Card takes more than one.
- Tests: `printPack.test.ts` (every shipped spray product x five last loads x three papers, plus fast-check over random products, tanks, loads, papers and locales: nothing dropped or doubled, decon first, core facts first, repeat notices on every card, every card within its estimate) and `tests/e2e/spray-card-print.spec.ts` over `/_dev/spray-print` (every product x five last loads x three papers in print media, no text or QR outside any card, part numbers contiguous, no fade line). In Chromium, WebKit and Firefox the fullest card measured 94% of its height on 2026-10-05.
- Typical set sizes for the 202 shipped spray products: 4x6 2 to 3 cards (3 to 4 with paraquat decon first), Letter 4-up 2 to 3 cells, 3x5 5 to 9 cards (more with decon first). 3x5 is small; the card page tells the farmer how many cards a paper takes.
