# Inputs plan for grain and forage crops: rulings for #720

Issue #720 said the inputs plan planned no herbicide for corn, soybeans or wheat and no fertility for wheat.
#777 and #809 fixed most of it. This document records the panel rulings (2026-10-09) that close the rest.
Each panel had three reviewers (safety, farmer usability, data provenance), and the majority decided.

## Sources read

- VCE Agronomy Handbook 2023 (424-100), Part I, Crop Descriptions
  (`spes-299-A.pdf`), the Fertilizer line for each crop.
- VT 424-026, Nitrogen Management for Winter Wheat. It gives timing in calendar
  terms only: dormancy "generally begins in December in Virginia", GS 25 "usually occurs in late
  February", and GS 30 at "the beginning of April".
- 2026 Virginia Pest Management Guide, Field Crops (456-016, `ENTO-633-E.pdf`):
  - Corn, p. 29, and Table 5.21 (p. 91).
  - Soybeans, p. 137, and Table 5.54 (p. 186).
  - Small grains, Tables 5.60 to 5.67 (pp. 203 to 221).
- EPA PPLS labels:
  - Glyphosate 4 Plus (81927-9, 2020-02-24).
  - Touchdown HiTech (100-1182, 2015-03-18).
  - Halex GT (100-1282, 2025-01-15).
  - Master Label 524-659, behind Roundup PowerMAX 3.

| R720-1 | Glyphosate over Roundup Ready corn and soybeans                                        | (b) 3-0 | Trait claims (`traitGatedSafeFor`, needs `glyphosate-tolerant-rr2`) only from real product labels: Glyphosate 4 Plus on `corn-feed-dent-pioneer` and `soybean-asgrow-roundup-ready-2-xtend`, and Touchdown HiTech on the corn only, because its soybean section names Roundup Ready 2 Yield, not Xtend. Roundup PowerMAX 3 gets no claim, because its PDF is a master label and its Roundup Ready text applies "only if Roundup Ready crops are included on the final printed label". Each claim is quoted under `traitGatedSafeFor` in `epa-reg-sources.json`, and `traitClaimSourceGaps` checks it (the product's own PPLS file, postemergence use, Roundup Ready or glyphosate-tolerant, the crop); Halex GT's existing claim is now quoted too. The planner's crop-safety filter passes crop traits and label claims to the unchanged kernel (`lib/safety` untouched, no `RULES_VERSION` bump). Glyphosate POST windows come from the VA PMG: 28-35 days after planting for corn ("four to five weeks"), 21-28 days for soybeans ("three to four weeks"). |
| R720-2 | Spring topdress for barley, rye and wheat                                              | (a) 2-1 | A `topdressN` entry may name a calendar window instead of a stage, applied the first time it comes after planting. The window comes from the source's own words (`sourceWords`): "early" is days 1-10, "mid" 11-20, "late" 21 to the end of the month, and a bare month is the whole month (`lib/plan/calendarWords.ts`). The gate checks that the quote contains the words and that the window is what they name. Filled: barley 80 lb and rye 60 lb "February or early March" (Feb 1 to Mar 10); SRW and HWW wheat 40 lb "late March" (Mar 21 to 31; lower end of 40-80). SRW wheat's Z30 stage anchor is gone. The rationale names the source words. The provenance reviewer voted for whole-month windows.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
