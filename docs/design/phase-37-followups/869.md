# #869 Photo marks: problem marks, a numbered print sheet and an offline copy

Follow-up to Phase 37F ([`PHASE_37_HERB_GARDEN_PLAN.md`](../PHASE_37_HERB_GARDEN_PLAN.md), rulings R-13, R-14, R-23 and R-24). No server migration, no new endpoint, no AI call beyond the existing `photo-help` path, no safety-rule change (`RULES_VERSION` unchanged). Dexie goes to v7.

## As built

**Guard (FU869-1 a, b).** `lib/journal/markGuard.ts` (pure, client-safe) adds two checks after the unchanged spray guard (`asksForSprayAdvice`, C-8):

- `namesDiagnosis(label, libraryTerms)` runs on **every** label, not only problem labels (decision D-1 below). It refuses `DIAGNOSIS_WORDS` (disease and pathogen words, pest and insect names, animal-damage names, common weed names, a few Spanish words) and the pesticide library's own target names: insecticide `targetPests` and scouting-threshold pests and fungicide `targetDiseases`, turned into match terms by `markDiagnosisTerms` (parentheticals and stage words such as "suppression" or "eggs" dropped). `markDiagnosisTermsFor(registry)` in `lib/server/sprayTerms.ts` builds them per registry view (cached), and the photo-help route passes them to `answerPhotoHelp` → `markedAnswer` → `parseMarks`. Terms match whole words, joined by a space, hyphen, underscore or nothing, singular or plural; the label is folded (case, accents) and also read through `normalizeQuestion`, so leetspeak and letter-by-letter spelling meet the same words.
- `namesTreatment(label)` runs on **problem** labels only. It refuses treatment and cure words, action verbs (remove, prune, cut, pinch, harvest, leave, feed, add, use, try, should, needs and the like), cause words (deficiency, nitrogen, overwatered, sunscald, frost damage, stress), and guesses (looks like, maybe, probably, because, caused by). An action mark still says what to do; a problem mark says only what is there.
- `parseMarks(raw, sprayTerms?, diagnosisTerms?)` gains the refusals `diagnosis-label` and `treatment-label`. Any refusal drops the whole set, and the answer falls back to steps only with "Claude could not place marks on this photo." as before.

**Prompt (c).** The marks rules in `aiPhotoHelp.ts` list `problem` as a kind, describe it as a pointer at something that looks wrong whose label and step say only what is visible, and forbid naming a pest, disease, weed, cause, product, treatment or cure in any label.

**Sheet (d, e).** `PhotoMarks.svelte` draws a problem point as a dashed diamond (a problem area as a dashed square-cornered box), its 48 px button as a rotated dashed square, and its legend badge the same, in a dark purple (`#4b1f73`, over 9:1 on white) with a white halo. With a problem mark the legend adds a key ("Circles and solid boxes: a step to take." / "Diamonds and dashed boxes: something that looks wrong. They do not say what it is.") and, below the steps, the crop's Care Guide "Common problems" items (from the snapshot's Care Guide card, spray-filtered, tagged `fallback` "Care guide" because they are the family tips that `careTips.sources.gate.test.ts` keeps sourced) or, when the crop has none, only the fixed extension office line. The app writes no symptom text. Strings are in `catalogs/{en,es}/herbsh.ts`.

**Fixture (g).** `aiFixture/photoMarks.ts` picks its answer by the grower's question: `FIXTURE_MARKS_PROBLEM` returns a cut mark and a problem area labelled "Yellow lower leaves"; `FIXTURE_MARKS_DIAGNOSIS` returns a problem label "Powdery mildew here", which the server refuses with the set. Phrases are in `lib/aiFixturePhrases.ts`.

**Print (FU869-2).** Each marked journal entry (live or saved copy) has a 48 px Print button. It opens the shared `Modal` with a paper choice (Letter portrait, 4x6 index card; 48 px rows) and an on-screen preview. Print fetches the photo as a data URL (the saved copy already is one) and decodes it, then sets `photoPrint.job` (`lib/journal/photoPrintJob.svelte.ts`); the Card page renders `PhotoMarksPrint.svelte` outside its `no-print` wrapper in place of `CardPrintSheet` until `afterprint`, and its own Print button clears the job first. The sheet is one SVG with the photo and the numbered circles and diamonds drawn on it at the photo's natural size (`markGeometry` keeps each badge inside the photo), the numbered "Kind: label" lines (`printMarkLines`, blank labels fall back to the kind word, then the number), Claude's steps on Letter only, the problem key and tips or extension line, the entry provenance, the date and "As of <time>" (the copy's save time offline, plus "Saved copy from <date>."). CSS in `lib/journal/photoPrint.css` uses the Card pages `cc-letter` and `cc-4x6`.

**Offline copy (FU869-3).** Dexie v7 adds `journalCopies` (`[ownerId+entryId], ownerId, [ownerId+cropId]`). `lib/client/journalCopyStore.ts` is the only reader and writer; `lib/journal/savedCopy.ts` defines and validates the stored shape (id, planting, date, question text, provenance, answer source, text, steps and marks, with the marks re-checked by `parseMarks`; no author, block, vault id, spray redirect or delete right). The photo is kept as a JPEG data URL (magic bytes checked, at most the 300 KB journal cap). After the journal list loads online, PhotoHelp drops copies of entries the list no longer holds (only those at least as new as the list's oldest entry when the list is cut at its 50-entry limit, `JOURNAL_LIST_LIMIT`), drops every copy of a planting answering 404, then refreshes each shown marked entry's copy or fetches its photo and saves it (a photo answering 404 drops the copy). A new answer is saved at once; an owner's delete forgets the copy. Trimming keeps the 20 newest per planting, then at most 100 and 15 MB per Owner, dropping the least recently saved first. Every call reads the active Owner from the same sessionStorage key as the card store and is refused when it is not the Owner the page was loaded for (a stale tab after a switch). `clearCardCaches()` clears the table, which covers an Owner switch and logout. When the live journal cannot load, the journal lists the saved copies read-only under "Saved on this phone", each with "Saved copy from <date>.", the redrawn overlay and Print.

## Decisions beyond the rulings

- **D-1.** The diagnosis check runs on every label, not only problem labels: the 37F prompt already forbade naming a disease or pest in a cut, pinch, harvest or leave label, and a cut label that names a disease is the same unsourced diagnosis. The treatment check runs on problem labels only, since the other kinds exist to say what to do.
- **D-2.** Problem labels are also refused for cause words and guesses (deficiency, overwatered, sunscald, looks like, maybe, because), as FU869-1 (c) forbids naming a cause. Generic "insect" and "bug" are refused too: they name a living cause.
- **D-3.** The 404 check uses the existing list and photo endpoints (no new single-entry GET). An old entry stored before #869 whose label now fails the stricter guard still shows online but is not saved for offline.
- **D-4.** The print sheet shows the problem tips on Letter only; a 4x6 card shows the problem key and, when the crop has no tips, the extension office line.
