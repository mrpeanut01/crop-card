# #867: Phase 37 herb data, second research pass (2026-10-20)

This pass worked the open items in [`docs/research/phase-37-herb-research-prompt.md`](../../research/phase-37-herb-research-prompt.md). Every page was fetched with `curl` and turned into text locally. Every quote that shipped was then checked two ways against that text:

- a substring match after normalising whitespace and quote marks;
- the repo's own `quoteFound` from `apps/web/scripts/lib/sourceDrift.mjs`.

Search results and page summaries were used only to find pages. Seed-company, nursery and blog pages were not counted. Clemson HGIC answers 403 behind a bot wall and was not used. Several `ucanr.edu` pages show a "Checking connection" wall and were not used either.

Sources are in `apps/web/scripts/crop-data-sources.json`, each with `url`, `publisher`, `date` and `quote`. The gates in `apps/web/src/lib/plugins/sourceCoverage.ts` pass.

## Other items

- **East Indian lemongrass `lifecycle`: filled.** Purdue NewCROP names "East Indian, Cymbopogon flexuosus" as a perennial herb, so `lifecycle.notRecorded` is gone and `SOURCED_LIFECYCLES` gains the entry.
- **East Indian lemongrass `winterHardyToZone`: still empty.** Purdue NewCROP and Wisconsin Horticulture name C. flexuosus but give no zone. Illinois and the NC State Plant Toolbox cover C. citratus only. The reason is updated in `winterHardyToZone.notRecorded`.
- **Thyme harvest timing: still empty (disagree).** Sources split two ways:
  - Before flowering: Illinois, Penn State, the NC State Plant Toolbox and Iowa State "All About Thyme" (which also allows "when the first blossoms begin to open").
  - At or in bloom: UMD ("when first blossoms open"), WSU C159 ("when in full bloom"), MU G6470 ("while in flower"), Montana State ("normally at bloom") and Purdue NewCROP ("during the bloom period").
- **Per-method timing hint ("cut before bloom" for drying): sources found, not built.** Several pages state the drying timing for a named herb:
  - basil: Purdue HO-189, "Where basil is grown for its dried leaves ... it is cut just prior to the appearance of flowers."
  - rosemary: Penn State, "Prior to blossoming, harvest leaves for drying."
  - mint: Illinois and UMD.

  The hint itself is app code: a `CardFact.note` on the harvest task plus a per-crop field or rule. That is outside a data pass, so it is left for a follow-up. Mint and sage already carry a drying care task.

- **`preserving.freeze-dry`: none.** No allowed page found names a specific herb.
- **`preserving.infuse` by vinegar: found, not shipped.** OSU SP 50-736 "Flavored Vinegars" names mint, basil, tarragon, dill, oregano, chives and nasturtiums. The infuse note and its safety line are written for oil and botulism. A vinegar infusion needs its own safety-line ruling before any herb ships `infuse` from a vinegar source.
- **Basil and Greek oregano drying: still empty.** The brief asks for a panel ruling on how good and fair are weighed. None was made here.
- **`sunNeed` (optional, R-19): not built.** It needs a schema field, a gate and a shortlist change. The research helper found candidate pages for most herbs (NC State Plant Toolbox, Illinois, Wisconsin, UMD, Utah State). Those quotes were not re-verified in this pass, so they are not listed as sources.

## Rulings made in this pass

| Id     | Ruling                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| F867-1 | A `harvestCadence` ships only from a source that describes repeated cuts through the season. The pantry repeats the cadence until frost or season end. A source that limits the number of cuts is recorded here and not shipped: cilantro goes to seed after its second cutting, and chamomile was raked three or four times. A pinching interval is not a harvest cadence either (stevia every 2 to 3 weeks; UF/IFAS Pasco's basil and chive "prune every 2-3 weeks" disagrees with other sources anyway). |
| F867-2 | A seasonal-row quote may join verbatim pieces with "..." from one section of a page about a single herb, when that section names the herb. This covers the NC State Plant Toolbox Description for hyssop and perilla. `waterNeed` keeps the stricter rule the brief set: the passage itself names the crop.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| F867-3 | A source's own freezing instruction for a named herb says freezing works for it (`good`). Penn State: "Chop chives and lemon grass before freezing." This answers the brief's open question on lemongrass. The ruled names count lemon grass for East Indian lemongrass, so the species question does not arise for preserving.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| F867-4 | A comparison such as "retain more flavor if frozen and not dried" (Kentucky State, lovage) supports the better method. It does not by itself rate the other method `fair` or `poor`, so lovage keeps UMass's drying.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| F867-5 | Text an extension page reprints from an industry or seed-company source does not count, such as UNL Lancaster's National Garden Bureau "Year of the Monarda". Reader comments under an extension blog post do not count either; only the article text does.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
