# Crop row spacing sources (#591)

Every `plantingGuide.rowSpacingIn` and `defaultRowSpacingInches` on a crop plugin, and the in-row spacing of every tree crop, now has a verbatim quote in `apps/web/scripts/crop-data-sources.json` or was removed. `rowSpacingGaps` in `apps/web/src/lib/plugins/sourceCoverage.ts` and `cropFactPaths` gate it: a source keyed by the field (an equal `defaultRowSpacingInches` shares the `rowSpacingIn` source) whose quote states the stored number (inches, feet, or feet as a word) and names rows; a tree crop's in-row quote states both ends of its range.

Result: 279 crops sourced, 71 left empty, 7 tree in-row ranges sourced and 2 removed.

## Panel rulings (2026-10-06)

Panel: farmer/agronomist, data integrity, precedent/engineering.

| #   | Question                                                   | Ruling                                                                                                                                     | Vote                              |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Q1  | The field holds one number; sources give a range           | Store the lower end of the between-row range (the minimum, as VCE 426-840 and #587 do); the quote must state it                            | 3-0                               |
| Q2  | A crop with no sourced row spacing                         | `rowSpacingOf` spaces rows square at the in-row midpoint, tagged `fallback`; with no in-row value either, the 12 in `fallback` placeholder | 3-0                               |
| Q3  | Named varieties, incl. mis-entered 3, 9 and 10.5 in values | Take the crop-type figure from an allowed source; seed-company pages do not count                                                          | 3-0                               |
| Q4a | Dry bush beans under VCE "Beans, bush"                     | Yes                                                                                                                                        | 3-0                               |
| Q4b | Bok choy under "Cabbage, Chinese"                          | Yes                                                                                                                                        | 3-0                               |
| Q4c | Galia and Charentais under "Muskmelon"                     | Yes                                                                                                                                        | 3-0                               |
| Q4d | Romanesco under "Broccoli"                                 | No; broccoli and cauliflower lower ends differ, so left empty                                                                              | 2-1 (one for the cauliflower row) |
| Q4e | Semierect Triple Crown blackberry                          | 96 in from 426-840's caneberry text "allow at least 8 feet between rows"                                                                   | 3-0                               |
| Q4f | Japanese-style sweet corn under "Corn, sweet"              | Yes                                                                                                                                        | 3-0                               |
| Q5  | `defaultRowSpacingInches` duplicating `rowSpacingIn`       | One `rowSpacingIn` source covers an equal value; a different value needs its own key                                                       | 2-1                               |
| Q6  | Strawberry: Table 1 minimum 3 ft vs text "2 to 3 feet"     | 36 in, the one figure both agree on                                                                                                        | 3-0                               |

Coordinator calls under these rulings and the #551 overlap rule (any non-overlapping counted source leaves the value empty): a research-trial plot layout or a study finding is not a recommendation and does not count; a hybrid (jostaberry, Illinois Everbearing mulberry) does not take a parent's figure; a source reachable only over http is refused by the source schema; trellised-only figures (VCE 426-406 cucumbers and melons) are not counted against untrellised rows; head lettuce follows UMD's type-specific figure (leaf, cos and butterhead 12-24 in; crisphead 20-30 in does not overlap VCE "Lettuce, head" 10-18 in, so iceberg is empty).
