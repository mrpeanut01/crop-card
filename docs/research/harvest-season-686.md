# Perennial harvest season sources (#686)

`harvestSeason` (`start` and `end` as `{ month, day }`) gives a perennial its yearly harvest window. `harvestSeasonQuoteGaps` in `apps/web/src/lib/plugins/sourceCoverage.ts` requires an extension or government source whose quote states both dates. Only `peach-redhaven` and `peach-elberta` carry one (VCE 422-762, Blacksburg dates as printed).

## Which perennials have no window

Families with a day-of-year template (`PERENNIAL_DAYOFYEAR_TEMPLATES` in `lib/plugins/growthStageTemplates.ts`) show a typical-timing window: `orchard`, `vine-fruit` (grapes and hops), `small-fruit` and `bramble`. Other perennial herbs and vegetables (rhubarb, alliums, roots, leafy greens, herbs) get a window counted from the planting date through their family stage table. Only two families have neither:

- `stone-fruit` without `harvestSeason`: `cherry-bing-sweet`, `cherry-rainier-sweet`, `cherry-tart-montmorency`, `plum-methley`, `plum-stanley`, `apricot-moorpark`
- `perennial-vegetable`: `asparagus-jersey-knight`, `asparagus-millennium`, `asparagus-purple-passion`

These show no harvest window.

## Panel rulings (2026-10-09, safety, farmer usability, data provenance)

| Id   | Question                                                                              | Vote        | Outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| HS-1 | Does an extension source from outside Virginia count?                                 | 3-0 for (a) | A quoted land-grant .edu or .gov source from Virginia or a neighbouring mid-Atlantic state counts, but only when no in-region source disagrees. A disagreement leaves the crop empty, as under the #551 and #600 overlap rule. Pacific Northwest, Deep South and nursery dates never count. Methley stays empty even if a day-level date appears, unless the UMD and NC State disagreement is settled. |
| HS-2 | Does a month phrase ("mid- to late-June", "mid-August") count as start and end dates? | 3-0 for (a) | No. Turning a phrase into days would invent numbers the source never printed and show them as `data`. The gate's month-and-day requirement stays. A future text-only display such as "Typically mid-August (UMD Extension)" would need its own ruling and field.                                                                                                                                       |
| HS-3 | Is "no window shown", with this record, enough to close #686?                         | 3-0 for (a) | Yes. The mechanism, the gate and the sourced peach windows shipped. Every other perennial has a window. Stone fruit and asparagus show none because no source dates them. A day-dated mid-Atlantic source for any of these crops is a new data item, not a reason to keep #686 open.                                                                                                                   |
