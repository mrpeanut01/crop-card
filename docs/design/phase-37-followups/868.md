# #868: pre-review of the Phase 37 Spanish

A first pass over the Spanish that Phase 37 (#865, #866) shipped, done before the owner's native-speaker proofreader reads it. This pass does not sign the Spanish off. It aligns the new strings with the vocabulary and register of the catalogs the proofreader already approved on 2026-10-03, fixes grammar and meaning slips, and lists the strings it is least sure of.

Scope: `apps/web/src/lib/i18n/catalogs/es/herbsa.ts` to `herbsf.ts`, the 20 herb rows in `es/seasonal.ts`, `onboard.startBrief` and the demo herb garden names in `es/entry.ts`, plus English still showing on the Phase 37 screens. No key was renamed. No English text changed except two new demo keys (below), so `seasonalTaskText` still matches every stored row.

## Result

- 83 Spanish values changed: herbsa 15, herbsb 4, herbsc 15, herbsd 3, herbse 28, herbsf 9, seasonal 7, entry 2.
- 2 new keys, in English and Spanish: `entry.demoFarm.s.bunch` and `entry.demoFarm.p.bunches`.
- Every placeholder in scope is the same in English and Spanish (checked by a one-off script over every key in scope).
- Register: every string uses tú, like the reviewed catalogs. No string addresses the reader as usted.

### Meaning and grammar

- Agreement. `herbsc.reason.fair.fresh` said "{crop} es solo regular usado fresco", which is masculine whatever the crop (it read "Albahaca … usado fresco"); it now reads "en fresco". `herbse.editor.gpmHint` said "ese Área" and now says "esa Área". `herbsd.push.bringIn` said "llévala … cúbrela", but the push can list several herbs; it now reads "lleva las plantas adentro o cúbrelas", which works for one name or several.
- Meaning. `herbsc.goal.freeze` and `freezeDry` named the goal with the same words as the finished product ("Congelado: Falta"); they are now Congelación and Liofilización. `herbsc.status.short` "Falta" read as "missing" and is now "No alcanza". The `infuse` reasons said "para infusiones", which reads as tea, but this goal is herb vinegars and oils, so they now say "para infusionar". `herbsc.preserve.infuseSafety` said "no safety source exists" rather than "safety guidance is not sourced yet". `herbse.timer.next` said the timer "da" 0.4 in and now says it "riega" 0.4 in, like the no-amount line. The `herbse.api.scheduleIrrigationOnly` error used "regador", which in much of Latin America means a person or a sprinkler; it now says "equipo de riego". `herbsb.line.tender` said "en las zonas 8a y más cálidas", a word-for-word copy of "zones 8a and warmer", and now says "en la zona 8a y en las más cálidas … llévala adentro" (the same "adentro" as the herb calendar). `herbsb.line.typedZone` now says "Se usa la zona que ingresaste: 7a."
- Wording. "Junto a una ventana" for windowsill, where "Una ventana" lost the sill. "suficiente orégano seco" keeps the English "enough". `herbsa.because.why.cooking` now reads "Elegida por ser básica en la cocina." `herbse.water.mixed` now names what follows the thirstiest plant (the advice). `herbse.targets.none` uses "vinculado" (linked), where "unido" meant joined, and keeps "count nowhere". `herbsc.sched.again` is now the noun "Otro corte", which pairs with "Primera cosecha". `herbsc.pantry.keepsBy` now reads "Cómo se conserva". The Area flags now say "Cultivo en macetas o contenedores" and "Dentro de casa, como junto a una ventana".
- Care tasks and seasonal rows. Both had doubled "para … para", a "Quítalo" with no masculine noun before it (the oregano mulch, now "Retira la cubierta"), "No cortes" where the reviewed rows say "No recortes" for cut back, "para podar" where the sense is "antes de podar", the marjoram line missing "dulce", "botones" without "florales", "lleguen a hacer semilla" where the other rows say "den semilla", "apretada" for crowded mint (now "amontonada") and commas before "y" or "o".

### English on the Phase 37 screens (fixed)

1. Photo help journal: the journal stores the preset question in English ("Where do I prune?", from `questionText`), and the sheet showed it as stored. `PhotoHelp.svelte` now shows the preset part in the reader's language through the existing `cardsui.photo.q.*` keys; notes and typed questions stay as typed. Test: `PhotoHelp.locale.svelte.test.ts`.
2. Photo help heading: "Sobre Basil — Genovese · Cama 1". For a planting with no variety name, `photoHelpTargets` used the plugin's English name; it now goes through `cropDisplayName`, so it reads "Sobre Albahaca Genovese · Cama 1", while a typed name stays as typed and English output is unchanged. Test added in `lib/journal/targets.test.ts`.
3. Demo pantry: "4 bunch", "1 bunch". The demo seeder wrote the herb harvests' quantity and unit without passing them through the demo localizer. They now go through `L()`, with the new keys `entry.demoFarm.s.bunch` (manojo) and `entry.demoFarm.p.bunches` ("{count} manojo"). Test added in `lib/demo/localize.test.ts`.

### Seen, not changed (outside this issue)

- `/today` suggestion "Harvest — Manzana Honeycrisp sobre G.41": the word "Harvest" in an orchard harvest-window calendar event title. That title predates Phase 37.
- Crop plugin text on the Planting and Area Cards and on /harvest ("Cut at 6 in", "Pre-flower harvest for peak essential oils", "Hardy perennial in zones 5+…"). This is plugin data, and it predates Phase 37.
- `/plan/pantry` "Schedule the next cut" posts the task title in the reader's language (`herbsc.task.title`), so a Spanish owner stores a Spanish title that an English helper then sees. Phase 37 shipped it this way. A fix would store English and translate on display, the way the herb calendar events do.
- The demo's sprayer alert "25 gal ATV/UTV-mount sprayer needs decon (fungicide)" stays in English. It may be English by rule (decon), and it is outside Phase 37.
- The `{time}` lines ("a las {time}") read "a las 1:00" at one o'clock, where Spanish needs "a la 1:00". One template cannot fix this; it needs a code change in `runWhenText` and `scheduleSummary`. It only shows for a 1:00 run.

## Crawl

A production build on port 5430 (`AUTH_MODE=direct`, `CROPCARD_LOCALES=en,es`, `E2E_CLAUDE_FIXTURE=1` with `ENABLE_DEV_ROUTES=1`) was crawled at 375 px wide with the `looksEnglish` heuristic from `apps/web/scripts/i18n-crawl.mjs`, in two passes:

- The Willow Run demo farm started in Spanish (`/demo?/start`, `es-MX`): `/today`, `/plan`, `/plan/brief`, `/plan/pantry`, `/plan/calendar`, `/harvest`, `/equipment`, the drip timer page with the schedule editor open, the herb garden designer, the herb garden Area Card, and the oregano and chives Planting Cards.
- The seeded owner with fixture Claude: a basil Planting Card, the photo help sheet with a photo, the "¿Dónde la podo?" answer with three marks and the legend, and `/plan/brief` before and after a chat turn.

Before this change, the crawl found English from Phase 37 in three places: the stored question "Where do I prune?" in the journal, the plugin name in the photo help heading, and "bunch" on the pantry. All three are fixed above. A second crawl after the fix shows "Sobre Albahaca Genovese · Cama 1", the saved question as "¿Dónde la podo?" and "4 manojo" on the pantry and on /harvest. No Phase 37 string reads as English on any crawled page.

Other English the crawl found is data or outside scope: farm and user names, plugin harvest cues and notes, the fixture Claude's canned English ("Mark one", "Fixture Claude reply…"), Spanish month abbreviations such as "ago" (agosto) that the heuristic counts as English, and the items listed under "Seen, not changed".
