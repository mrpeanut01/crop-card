# Inputs plan for grain and forage crops: rulings for #720

Issue #720 said the inputs plan planned no herbicide for corn, soybeans or wheat and no fertility for wheat.
#777 and #809 fixed most of it. This document records the panel rulings (2026-10-09) that close the rest.
Each panel had three reviewers (safety, farmer usability, data provenance), and the majority decided.

## Sources read

- VCE Agronomy Handbook 2023 (424-100), Part I, Crop Descriptions
  (`spes-299-A.pdf`), the Fertilizer line for each crop.
- VT 424-026, Nitrogen Management for Winter Wheat. It gives timing in calendar
  terms only: dormancy "generally begins in December in Virginia", GS 25 "usually occurs in late
  February", and GS 30 at "the beginning of April".
- 2026 Virginia Pest Management Guide, Field Crops (456-016, `ENTO-633-E.pdf`):
  - Corn, p. 29, and Table 5.21 (p. 91).
  - Soybeans, p. 137, and Table 5.54 (p. 186).
  - Small grains, Tables 5.60 to 5.67 (pp. 203 to 221).
- EPA PPLS labels:
  - Glyphosate 4 Plus (81927-9, 2020-02-24).
  - Touchdown HiTech (100-1182, 2015-03-18).
  - Halex GT (100-1282, 2025-01-15).
  - Master Label 524-659, behind Roundup PowerMAX 3.

## Corn N need in the inputs plan (#739, 2026-10-17)

A crop's nitrogen need lived in two separately sourced plugin fields: `fertility.preplant` and `topdressN` (#809, read by the Inputs plan through `fertilityBudgetFor`) and `nitrogenNeedLbPerAcre` (#811, the /fertility "N need" row). Seven crops carry `nitrogenNeedLbPerAcre`. Five of them (both winter wheats, barley, rye, soybean) also carry a `fertility` block, and their planned pre-plant N plus topdress lands inside the sourced need range. `corn` and `corn-feed-dent-pioneer` carry only the need, 125-150 lb N per acre from VCE 424-100 ("For grain, 125-150 lbs N"). The Inputs plan budgeted them from `FAMILY_REMOVAL_DEFAULTS.corn` instead: 150 lb N pre-plant tagged `fallback`, plus an unsourced 40 lb family sidedress, so the farmer saw 190 lb N planned beside a sourced 125-150 for the same planting. A panel of three (agronomy safety, data provenance, farmer usability) weighed three options: (a) `fertilityBudgetFor` falls back to `nitrogenNeedLbPerAcre` when a crop has no `fertility` block; (b) declare one field canonical and derive or remove the other; (c) leave both and show the mismatch. N739-1 to N739-4 changed no plugin data; N739-5 adds corn's sourced `fertility.preplant`. `lib/safety` is untouched, so `RULES_VERSION` is unchanged.

| N739-1 | Which option?                                               | (a) 3-0 | A crop with a `fertility` block keeps it, because only that block carries the pre-plant and topdress split. A crop with no `fertility` block but a sourced `nitrogenNeedLbPerAcre` plans the lower end of the summed parts as its N (#591 Q1, as R720-7), tagged `data`. Safety: the sourced figure is lower than the family estimate, and the planner should never plan more N than the source names. Provenance: a quoted figure always beats an unsourced default, and every N the planner shows can now be traced. Usability: the two screens agree. Option (b) was rejected because the fields answer different questions: a season total versus a dated split. Retiring either one loses data or invents a split. Option (c) was rejected because showing two numbers for one planting and asking the farmer to pick is the confusion this panel exists to remove. |
| N739-2 | P and K for a crop whose N comes from its need              | (b) 2-1 | P₂O₅ and K₂O stay on the family default and stay `fallback`. `nitrogenNeedLbPerAcre` is an N-only field, and this ruling adds no agronomy numbers to any plugin. Dissent (provenance): the same VCE sentence states "Apply 40-60 lbs P2O5 and 40-60 lbs K2O at medium soil test levels", and grain sorghum already takes its `fertility.preplant` from it, so corn should get a full block. The owner upheld the dissent on 2026-10-17 (N739-5): both corn plugins now carry that sourced `fertility.preplant`, which supersedes this fallback by N739-1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| N739-3 | How is a budget with a sourced N and typical P and K shown? | 3-0     | The application keeps `budgetProvenance: 'data'` (the N drives the product choice and the amount), and carries `nutrientProvenance` per nutrient and the `nitrogenNeed` range. The Inputs step shows a `data` chip ("extension figure", the same label as /fertility) with "Nitrogen is the low end of the extension figure for this crop, 125–150 lb/ac of N." and a `fallback` chip with "P₂O₅, K₂O: typical amount for this kind of crop, not from a source. Adjust it to your soil test." (en and es). The rationale says "N from the extension N need, lower end; P₂O₅ and K₂O typical estimate, not from a source". No single tag claims more than its nutrient supports.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| N739-4 | Does the family sidedress still go on top?                  | (b) 2-1 | No. VCE gives 125-150 lb as the season's N, not a pre-plant amount, so the unsourced 40 lb family sidedress on top would plan 165 lb, past the source's upper bound. This matches the existing rule that a crop with its own budget gets no family sidedress (grain sorghum). The whole sourced N is planned pre-plant, before soil and cover-crop credits. Dissent (usability): Virginia corn growers usually split N, so keep the sidedress date and move part of the 125 lb there. Safety and provenance voted against it: no source states the split, and R720-3 says the planner never times anything from an unsourced stage table. A sourced split would come in as `topdressN` in a corn `fertility` block.                                                                                                                                                      |
| N739-5 | Owner ruling (2026-10-17): corn's P and K                   | Owner   | The N739-2 dissent is upheld. This is an owner ruling, not a panel vote. `corn` and `corn-feed-dent-pioneer` get a `fertility.preplant` from the same VCE 424-100 sentence that gives their N need: "Fertilizer For grain, 125-150 lbs N. Apply 40-60 lbs P2O5 and 40-60 lbs K2O at medium soil test levels." Each value is the lower end of its range (#591 Q1, as N739-1 and grain sorghum's block from the same sentence): N 125, P₂O₅ 40, K₂O 40 lb per acre, each quoted under `fertility.preplant.*` in `crop-data-sources.json`. No `topdressN` or split is added, because the source states none (N739-4). `nitrogenNeedLbPerAcre` stays for the /fertility N need row. By N739-1 the block now wins, so corn's budget is all `data` and no shipped crop takes the need-only path.                                                                               |

Tests: `inputsPlan.grainCrops.test.ts` checks that corn's budget is its `fertility` block (125 lb N, 40 lb P₂O₅, 40 lb K₂O, all `data`) with no sidedress, that wheat's `fertility` block is unchanged, that a crop with neither field still plans the family default as `fallback`, that no shipped crop is need-only and a fixture corn without its block still plans the low end of its need tagged `data` with typical P and K and agrees with the /fertility N need row, and that every crop carrying both fields (corn included) plans N inside its need range. The W842 ranking tests in `inputsPlan.organicRanking.test.ts` strip corn's `fertility` block and need from their fixture, because they use corn only to get a pre-plant slot and a sidedress slot.
